# Prohibited goods (draft)

Merchants must not list:

- Illegal goods or services
- Infringing or pirated content
- Cheats, unauthorized bots, or malware
- Account trading for third-party platforms
- Traffic, ranking, or metric manipulation
- Unofficial virtual currency or unofficial branded digital items the merchant is not licensed to sell

The application enforces keyword and category holds. Enforcement in software does not replace the merchant’s legal duty.
