# Product scope

Working name: **Independent Mall** (temporary; not a registered brand).

Independent Mall is a general-purpose storefront for lawful goods and services:

- Physical goods
- Own-brand goods
- Digitally delivered goods that the merchant is authorized to sell
- Software subscriptions and memberships
- Courses, design, consulting, and similar lawful services

It is not a traffic-boosting, account-trading, cheat, or unofficial virtual-currency platform. Those categories are rejected by product policy, not merely hidden.

## Community Edition

A complete shop that installs and runs without buying a license and without contacting any official license server. Core flows: browse, cart, checkout, fake/sandbox payment, order management, digital delivery, refunds requests, coupons, membership basics, audit, prohibited-goods rules, and product review.

## Commercial Edition

Separately packaged modules: multi-store, advanced analytics, tickets, backups, official themes, plugin directory, paid payment adapters, auto-update, and support. Enabling those modules must not be a boolean buried in Community Core.

## Out of scope for this product

- Reselling another vendor’s obfuscated shop
- Bundled third-party AppIDs or payment merchant IDs
- Remote kill-switch, remote database wipe, or undisclosed telemetry
- Default support for prohibited listings

Legal templates in `docs/legal/` are drafts. They must be reviewed by a PRC-qualified lawyer before public use.
